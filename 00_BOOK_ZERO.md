string `Book Zero` appears **84 times** across the nine governance documents. `00_READ_FIRST.md`
Rule 10 reserves questions of theology, ethics, and foundational human values **exclusively** to

inherited from a source no reviewer could inspect. Rule 5 traceability could not be completed.
Rule 10's reserved home could not be opened.

reduce Book Zero.**
